THE MAN BEHIND THE NUMBER 10

Biography & Early Life

Full name: Lionel Andrés Messi Cuccittini

Born: 24 June 1987, Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina.

Position: Forward / attacking playmaker

Preferred foot: Left

Messi developed his football talent in Rosario before joining FC Barcelona's youth system, La Masia, as a teenager. He made his official first-team debut in 2004 and became one of the most influential players in the club's history.

After leaving Barcelona in 2021, he played for Paris Saint-Germain and then joined Inter Miami in 2023. With Argentina, he won the 2021 and 2024 Copa América tournaments and the 2022 FIFA World Cup.
